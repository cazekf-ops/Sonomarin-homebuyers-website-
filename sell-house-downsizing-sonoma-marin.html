Downsizing Shouldn't Mean Months of Stress

Whether you're moving to a smaller place, closer to family, or into a community that better fits this stage of life, selling the family home doesn't have to mean a big renovation project or weeks of sorting through decades of belongings under pressure. We buy homes exactly as they are, so downsizing can be as simple as it should be.

We're Evan and Alex — local, licensed buyers based in Sonoma County. We've worked with many homeowners making this exact transition, often alongside their adult children or a trusted advisor helping them through it. There's no pressure here — just an honest, fair offer and a process that moves at whatever pace feels right to you.

You Don't Need to Clean Anything Out

This is often the biggest worry people have: what to do with everything that's accumulated over years or decades in a home. The honest answer is — nothing, if you don't want to. Take whatever's meaningful to you and leave the rest. Furniture, belongings, anything left behind, we handle it all after closing. There's no dumpster to rent, no estate sale to organize, no deadline to have the house "presentable."

No Updates or Repairs Needed

Many homes that have been lived in for decades need updating to compete on the retail market — a new kitchen, updated bathrooms, fresh flooring. That's a significant investment of both money and time that a traditional sale often requires. We buy homes as-is, so none of that is necessary. The house doesn't need to look like a magazine spread; it just needs to be yours to sell.

Family involved in the decision? We're glad to loop in adult children, a power of attorney, or anyone else helping you through this. Whatever makes the process feel most comfortable and clear for you is how we'll handle it.

Common Questions

Downsizing FAQs — Sonoma & Marin County

Do I need to clean out the house before selling?

No. You're welcome to take whatever's meaningful to you and leave the rest — furniture, belongings, decades of accumulated items. We handle cleanout and disposal after closing, so sorting through a lifetime of belongings under time pressure isn't something you need to do.

Can you help if I'm moving into assisted living or in with family?

Yes. We work with sellers making exactly this kind of transition regularly. We can move at whatever pace fits your situation, and we're glad to coordinate with family members or a trusted advisor helping you through the process.

The house needs updating — do I need to renovate before selling?

No. We buy homes as-is, including houses that haven't been updated in decades. There's no need to remodel a kitchen or bathroom, repaint, or make any improvements before selling to us.

Will selling to a cash buyer get me less than listing on the market?

It depends on your priorities. If the home needs significant updating and you have time to wait, listing on the market might net more — and as licensed agents, we'll tell you that honestly. If a simple, fast, low-stress sale matters more to you than maximizing every dollar, a direct cash sale is usually the better fit.

Can my adult children or a family member help manage the sale for me?

Absolutely. We're happy to communicate with a family member, power of attorney, or trusted advisor throughout the process, whatever makes you most comfortable.

How fast does this need to happen?

Entirely up to you. We can close in as little as 7 days, or take as much time as you need to plan your next move comfortably.
