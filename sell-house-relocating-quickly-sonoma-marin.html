Relocating Fast? We Can Match Your Timeline

A new job, military orders, or a family situation that requires a quick move doesn't leave much room for a drawn-out home sale. Showings, staging, and waiting on a buyer's financing can take months you simply don't have. We're built for exactly this situation.

We're Evan and Alex — local, licensed buyers based in Sonoma County. We work with relocating sellers regularly, including people who've already left the area, and we coordinate closings to fit real deadlines rather than the other way around.

Why Speed Matters When You're Relocating

  • Avoid carrying two mortgages. If you're buying in your new location before your Sonoma or Marin home sells, a fast, certain cash sale protects you from double payments.
  • No financing fall-throughs. Traditional buyers can back out late if their loan doesn't come through. We pay cash, so there's no financing contingency to worry about.
  • Skip showings entirely. No open houses, no last-minute walkthroughs to schedule around your move.
  • One less thing to manage from a distance. If you've already relocated, coordinating a traditional sale remotely is genuinely difficult.

Already Moved? We Handle That All the Time.

If you've already relocated out of state, you don't need to fly back to close. Paperwork can typically be handled through the title company remotely — including mobile notary services and mail-away closing packages — so the sale can wrap up without you needing to be physically present in California.

Need a little more time, not less? Speed works both ways — if you need extra time to coordinate your move rather than closing immediately, we can work around that too, including a short rent-back period in some cases so you can stay a bit longer after closing.

Relocation FAQs — Sonoma & Marin County

How fast can you close if I need to relocate for a new job?

We can close in as little as 7 days once the title is clear. If your relocation has a firm start date, tell us up front and we'll work to line up closing with your timeline.

Can you buy my house if I've already moved out of state?

Yes. We regularly work with sellers who have already relocated. Paperwork can be handled remotely through the title company, including mobile notary and mail-away closing options, so you don't need to fly back to Sonoma or Marin County to complete the sale.

What if I need a little extra time before I can hand over the house?

That's not a problem. While we can close in as little as 7 days, we can also close later if you need more time to coordinate your move, and in some cases arrange a short rent-back period so you can stay a bit longer after closing.

Do I need to make repairs or stage the house before I move?

No. We buy homes as-is, so there's no need to fix anything up, deep clean, or stage the property before you go. Leave what you don't want to take with you.

Will I end up paying two mortgages if I buy before I sell?

A fast, certain cash sale is one of the most effective ways to avoid carrying two mortgages during a relocation. Because there's no financing contingency and no waiting on a buyer's loan approval, you can lock in your closing date with confidence and plan your next move around it.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. Zero fees, zero commissions, and we cover normal closing costs. The offer we make is the amount you walk away with.
