About This Property

This 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offers 1,614 square feet on a generous 0.43-acre lot in Mill Valley. The house is fully livable in its current condition — the roof and water heater are both in good shape — but it needs foundation work and updating throughout to reach its full potential. It will be delivered vacant.

The Opportunity

This is not a straightforward flip. The real upside here is structural, and it takes a buyer willing to think beyond a cosmetic renovation to see it.

Lot Split Potential

At 0.43 acres, this lot may support a split, subject to Mill Valley's zoning and subdivision requirements. A buyer who confirms feasibility with the city could unlock a second buildable parcel from a single acquisition.

Add Square Footage, Not Just Value

A meaningful portion of the property's footprint is underutilized space that could be converted or built out. Construction in this range is estimated at $400–$500 per square foot — and in a neighborhood where finished square footage commands $1,000+ per square foot, that gap is where the real return lives. Adding livable square footage here isn't just a repair — it's a value-creation play in its own right, on top of the renovation itself.

For a buyer willing to execute on either angle — or both — this property offers meaningfully more upside than the surface numbers suggest.
