A Damaged or Rundown House Doesn't Mean a Hard Sale

A home that needs major work — whether it's years of deferred maintenance, storm and water damage, or the aftermath of a fire — can feel impossible to sell the traditional way. Lenders often won't finance homes with serious issues, retail buyers get spooked by inspection reports, and the cost and hassle of doing the repairs yourself can be overwhelming. That's exactly the kind of property we specialize in.

We're Evan and Alex — local, licensed buyers based in Sonoma County. We buy homes in any condition, and we've purchased properties with everything from minor cosmetic issues to significant fire and water damage. There's no judgment and no pressure — just an honest, fair cash offer for your home as it sits today.

The Kinds of Damage and Repairs We Buy

  • Fire damage — from smoke damage to homes that have lost a room or more
  • Water damage — flooding, leaks, burst pipes, or long-term moisture issues
  • Mold — visible mold or a history of moisture problems
  • Foundation and structural issues — cracks, settling, or other structural concerns
  • Roof, plumbing, or electrical problems — including systems that are simply outdated
  • Unpermitted work or code violations — additions or renovations without proper permits
  • Hoarder homes or heavily cluttered properties — leave everything, we handle it
  • General deferred maintenance — homes that just need more work than you can take on

If your home has an issue not listed here, reach out anyway — we've likely seen it before.

Fire and Water Damage: The Insurance Question

If your home has fire or water damage, you may still be in the middle of an insurance claim — and that's okay. You don't need to wait until the claim is fully resolved to sell. In many cases, we can purchase the property with an open claim, and the remaining claim proceeds can sometimes be assigned or credited as part of the sale. We're familiar with this process and can work directly with your insurer or adjuster to keep things moving.

Sonoma County has real fire risk. Wildfires like the 2017 Tubbs Fire and 2020 Glass Fire affected thousands of local homes. If your property was damaged in a wildfire or other disaster, you're far from alone — and a fast, straightforward sale is often the least stressful path forward.

Why Selling As-Is Beats Repairing or Listing

  • Skip the repair costs. Major repairs can run tens of thousands of dollars — money you don't have to spend.
  • Skip the contractor delays. Getting quality contractors scheduled in Sonoma and Marin can take months, especially after regional disasters when demand spikes.
  • Avoid financing fall-throughs. Many lenders won't approve loans on homes with major damage, so a financed buyer's deal can collapse at the last minute. We pay cash — no financing contingency.
  • No inspection surprises. There's no retail inspection report to renegotiate around after you've already accepted an offer.

Common Questions

Repairs & Damage FAQs — Sonoma & Marin County

Do you buy houses with fire damage?

Yes. We regularly buy homes with fire damage, from smoke damage to homes that have lost a room or more to fire. You don't need to complete repairs or finish an insurance claim first — we can factor an open claim into the offer and work with your insurer directly if needed.

Do I need to finish my insurance claim before selling a fire or water damaged home?

No. We can purchase the property with an open claim, and in many cases the remaining claim proceeds can be assigned or credited as part of the sale. We'll walk you through the specifics based on your policy and claim status.

What if my house has mold, foundation issues, or unpermitted work?

We buy homes with all of these issues. Mold, foundation problems, and unpermitted additions are common reasons traditional buyers and lenders walk away — which is exactly the kind of property we specialize in. No inspections or disclosures beyond what's required by law are needed on your end.

Will I get a lower offer because my house needs a lot of work?

Our offer reflects the home's current condition and the cost of the repairs we'll take on after closing — that's standard for any as-is cash sale. But you avoid paying for those repairs yourself, avoid contractor delays, and avoid the risk that a retail buyer's inspection blows up the deal later. We're transparent about how we arrive at the number.

Do I need to clean out the house or remove debris first?

No. Leave the property exactly as it is, including fire debris, damaged belongings, or years of deferred maintenance. We handle all cleanup and hauling after closing.

How fast can you close on a damaged property?

As little as 7 days once the title is clear, or on whatever timeline works for you — including coordinating around an active insurance claim if needed.
